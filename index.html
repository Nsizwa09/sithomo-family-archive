Mbatha oShandu kaNdaba

Isibongo sakwaSithomo sisuka kwaMbatha, oShandu kaNdaba. Kwathi lapho enye yamadodana eNkosi uMbatha ikhulelisa udade wabo, iNkosi yayikhipha kwaMbatha — yathi mayibizwe ngokuthi uSithomo, ngokuba yathomeka yakhulelisa ekhaya. Yileso sizathu esazala isibongo esisaziwa namuhla.

Ukuzalwa Komntwana KwaSithomo

Uma kuzelwe ingane kwaSithomo, kuthathwa inonga (inkaba) yengane, ifakwe emsamo, ezintungeni zophahla — noma kugujwe ubonda khona emsamo, ifakwe khona, kuvalwe. Lolu usiko luhlonipha indawo yasekhaya njengesizinda somuntu ozelwe, luhlanganise ingane nomndeni kanye nabadala.

Ukuhlanganiswa Komlando

Umlando Nomndeni

Lomlando wahlanganiswa yilaba abalandelayo, ngosizo lomkhulu nodawewabo womkhulu abanolwazi olunzulu ngomlando wesibongo: uMsizi, uMthenjwa, uLindokuhle noTholinhlanhla Sithomo. Amakhehla avelayo ayisikhombisa yilawa alandelayo — cinga igama ukuze uthole nomuphi umuntu noma uluhlu lwakhe.

Umthetho Sisekelo Wehlangano

Qhulaza Burial Scheme

Inhlangano yakhiwe ngamalungu omndeni noma isibongo sakwaSithomo. Wonke umuntu wakwaSithomo wamukelekile ukujoyina.

Ubulungu Nemithetho

  • Ilungu ngalinye livumelekile ukuba lifake / libhale abantu abangaphezu kweshumi (10), bengaba ngaphansi kweshumi.
  • Wonke umuntu oyilungu kumele alethe imininingwane yabantu abakhavile — amagama, izinombolo zezitifiketi noma omazisi.
  • Umuntu ongekho ohlwini olulethwe yilungu akayukukhokhelwa uma kuvela isifo.
  • Ukujoyina yi-R120, bese kukhokhwa i-R40 njalo ngenyanga, njalonjalo ukuze ubulungu bungaphazamiseki.
  • Uma kuvela isifo, kukhishwa i-R2000, uhanjiswe kulelo khaya elivelwe isifo.
  • Imali iyobukezwa ngokuqhubeka kwesikhathi, uma inhlangano isisimeme ngokwanele ezimalini.
  • Ubulungu buyaphela uma lingakhokhi. Ovelelwe isifo uzokhokhelwa ngaphansi kwemibandela ebalwe ngenhla; ongekho ebhukwini akayukukhokhelwa.
